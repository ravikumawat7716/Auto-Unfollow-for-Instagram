# Auto Unfollow for Instagram: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Auto Unfollow for Instagram is a Chrome extension that helps you find Instagram accounts that don't follow you back, and unfollow them.

## What it reads

When you press **Scan**, the extension reads the following from instagram.com, using the Instagram session already open in your browser:

* your Instagram user ID and username
* your follower and following counts
* the list of accounts you follow and the list of accounts that follow you (user ID, username, full name, verified flag, private flag)

## Where it is stored

Everything is saved only in your browser, in `chrome.storage.local`. This includes the lists above, your settings, your keep list and a log of what the extension did. The Excel report is made in your browser and saved to your computer only when you download it.

## What is shared

Nothing. The extension has no server. It doesn't collect analytics, doesn't use cookies of its own, and sends no data to the developer or any third party. The only network requests it makes go to instagram.com, from your browser, as you.

## Permissions

* **storage**: saves your scan, settings and log in your browser.
* **instagram.com host access**: runs the panel on instagram.com, reads your lists and clicks Unfollow for you.

## Deleting your data

Remove the extension from `chrome://extensions` and Chrome deletes everything it stored.
